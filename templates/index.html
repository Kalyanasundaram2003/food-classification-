Welcome to Food Classification and Nutritional System

This project uses deep learning to classify food images and provide detailed nutritional information for each item. By uploading an image, you’ll get instant predictions and nutrition facts per serving.

It helps users make informed dietary decisions and promotes healthy eating habits. The system is designed to support 15 common food items with accurate data mapping
